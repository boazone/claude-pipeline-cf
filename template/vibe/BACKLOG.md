# Backlog

> **Terminology.** This document uses the locked vocabulary of [`TERMINOLOGY.md`](TERMINOLOGY.md).

Things that were **proposed, considered and deliberately left out** — with the reason, and with what
would bring them back. It is not a wish list. An item earns a row here by having been thought about
once already, so that it is not re-derived from scratch and not silently forgotten. A bug found
during a story and not fixed in it lands here too.
